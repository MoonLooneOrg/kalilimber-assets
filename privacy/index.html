Privacy Policy — KaliLimber

Last updated: 8 August 2026 · Applies to the KaliLimber Android app (com.moonloone.kalilimber) · Publisher: MoonLoone
Русская версия

KaliLimber is a free app for learning the kalimba. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no ads. This policy explains what the app does with data, in plain terms.

Short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any other identifying information. The nickname you type on the welcome screen stays on your device.
  • The microphone is used only to recognise the notes you play, in real time. Audio is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.
  • Your favourites, learning progress, settings, and tuning calibration are stored on your device only and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • The app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase so we can fix bugs and see which parts of the app people use.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Microphone

KaliLimber needs the RECORD_AUDIO permission for two features:

  • Learn / Challenge — recognising whether you played the right note.
  • Tuner — measuring the pitch of each kalimba tine so it can be calibrated.

How it works: the microphone stream is analysed on your device, frame by frame, and immediately discarded. Only the detected note (for example "C4") is used by the app. Nothing is written to disk and nothing leaves the device. Audio is captured only while a Learn, Challenge, or Tuner session is open, and stops when you leave the screen.

You can decline the microphone permission or revoke it later in Android settings. The rest of the app keeps working; only note recognition and the tuner become unavailable.

Data stored on your device

The following is kept locally (Android DataStore / app database) and never uploaded:

DataWhy
Nickname you enter on the welcome screenTo greet you on the profile screen
Favourite songs and articlesSo you can find them again
Learning progress per songTo resume where you stopped
Settings: colour palette, notation, languageTo keep your preferences
Kalimba tuning calibrationTo adapt note recognition to your instrument
Cached songs and articlesSo the app works without a connection after the first load

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. The app also opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so this data is not copied off your device by the system.

Data sent to third parties

KaliLimber uses Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) as a service provider:

  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage events: which screens are opened, which buttons are tapped, which song was started or finished, and technical context Firebase adds automatically (app version, device model, OS version, country, and a randomly generated app-instance ID). We do not set any user identifier and do not send your nickname.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reports: stack trace, device model, OS version, app version. Sent only when the app crashes or a handled error occurs.
  • Cloud Firestore — the app downloads songs, lessons, and articles from our content database. This is a read-only request; no personal data is sent with it.

Google processes this data on our behalf. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

We do not use advertising SDKs, and we do not sell or share personal data with third parties for advertising or profiling.

Legal basis and retention

Where the GDPR applies, our legal basis for analytics and crash reporting is our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it (Art. 6(1)(f)). Analytics and crash data are retained according to Firebase's default retention settings; local data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Your rights

Because the app has no account, we hold no record that can be linked to you personally. You can:

  • delete all local data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings;
  • revoke the microphone permission at any time in Android settings;
  • contact us with questions or a data request at the address below.

Children

KaliLimber is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will remove it.
